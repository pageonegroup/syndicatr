The Problem

Releasing a story is harder than writing one.

01
100 publishers means 100 separate conversations
Want your story in 50 publications? That's 50 relationships to maintain, 50 pitches to send, 50 follow-ups to chase. And if you don't already know these people, your press release goes nowhere.
02
Your story goes live before you're ready
Publishers operate on their own schedules. One goes live Monday, another Friday. There's no unified moment. No momentum. By the time your last publication runs it, the news cycle has already moved on.
03
Getting the links is a job in itself
After everything goes live, you still have to track down proof from every single publisher — different people, different timelines, some you never hear back from. Reporting becomes a second project.
04
Stories leak before they're supposed to
The more people involved in a release, the more chances the story escapes early. Embargoes break. Exclusives get spoiled. The announcement you've been planning for weeks lands with none of the impact it deserved.
Syndicatr.

One submission. We handle the publishers, the timing, the distribution, and the proof. You stay focused on the story.

What You Get
Everything the old way
never gave you.
1
Access to a curated publication network
No cold emails. No introductions needed. Syndicatr is already the relationship. Submit once and your content reaches a wide network of publications across print, online, and broadcast — without you managing a single contact.
"We used to spend weeks just getting our story placed in three outlets. Now it goes to dozens, in days."
2
You control the timing, not the publishers
Set your preferred publish date and let Syndicatr manage the schedule. No more stories going live on someone else's clock. Your announcement, on your terms.
Preferred date is locked in at submission. Publishers follow your pacing, not theirs.
3
All your live links, in one dashboard
Every published article — every URL from every publisher — is consolidated into your dashboard the moment it goes live. No chasing. No spreadsheets. No waiting on people to get back to you. It's just there.
Links added by publishers appear in your dashboard instantly. Share them with your team or clients in seconds.
4
Distribution choices that match your campaign
Let Syndicatr choose the best-fit publications for your content, or pick your own from the network. Filter by type — editorial, social pages, broadcast, lifestyle — and see credit costs before you commit.
"Choose for Me" or handpick every outlet. Either way, you see exactly what it costs before anything goes out.
5
Monthly report with media value in PHP
At the end of each month, get a clean exportable report showing every published article, its live link, pageviews, and the PHP media value assigned by our partner Media Meter team. Proof of what your coverage was actually worth.
Turn a folder of links into a PHP-denominated business case for every stakeholder who needs to see it.

You will be notified of any approved change requests through in-platform notifications. No changes will be made without your explicit acknowledgment.
